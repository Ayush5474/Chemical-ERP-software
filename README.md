# Chemical-ERP-software
The Inventory ERP tracks raw materials and packaging from the day they arrive, through production, to finished goods and sale. Every quantity is tracked by batch, so you always know what you have, where it came from and what it cost. 
What you can do: 

1. Receive raw material and packaging stock in batches 
2. See stock batch-wise, supplier-wise and product-wise, with a full ledger 
4. Run production using lot-based formulas, with automatic material calculation and costing 
5. Compare input with output to see evaporation and wastage 
6. Keep finished goods stock and record sales with profit or loss 
7. Export reports to Excel and PDF
